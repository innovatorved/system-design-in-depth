# Editorial & Provenance Policy

**Project:** System Design in Depth  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 3 October 2026  

---

## 1. Primary-Source First Policy

All technical claims, formulas, protocol behaviors, and architectural comparisons must be derived from and verified against primary or authoritative engineering sources:
- **Peer-Reviewed Research Papers:** ACM, IEEE, USENIX, VLDB, and SOSP publications.
- **Formal Standards & RFCs:** IETF RFCs (HTTP, TCP, TLS, WebSockets) and W3C recommendations.
- **Official Documentation & Engineering Blogs:** Direct publications from teams that authored the systems (e.g., PostgreSQL docs, Kafka docs, Stripe Engineering, Discord Engineering, AWS Architecture Center).
- **Seminal Textbooks:** *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (Kleppmann), *Database Internals* (Petrov), *Site Reliability Engineering* (Beyer et al.).

Secondary summaries, interview cheat sheets, and commercial course materials may not be used as the authoritative basis for claims or structural outlines.

---

## 2. Artificial Intelligence Transparency & Verification

AI models may be utilized to assist with drafting prose, formatting diagrams, or compiling quiz variations under strict rules:
1. **No Proprietary Prompts:** Prompts must not ingest copyrighted or proprietary course outlines, paywalled text, or competitor curricula as templates.
2. **Mandatory Human Verification:** Every technical assertion, code sample, numerical formula, and architectural diagram generated with AI assistance must be reviewed for technical accuracy and alignment with cited sources.
3. **No Paraphrasing of External Curricula:** Re-wording or paraphrasing another work section-by-section is strictly prohibited. Concepts must be structured independently based on prerequisite analysis.

---

## 3. Case Study & Company Disclosures

Case studies analyzing private commercial architectures (e.g., Instagram, Discord, Stripe, Amazon Dynamo, GitLab) are **educational reconstructions** based strictly on public talks, papers, and postmortem incident reports. They do not claim access to proprietary internal source code or real-time production configurations.

---

## 4. Error Reporting & Provenance Challenges

We maintain zero tolerance for copyright infringements, unattributed reproduction, or technical inaccuracies. Community members may report concerns via GitHub:
- Technical or factual errors: Open a [Factual Error Report](https://github.com/innovatorved/system-design-in-depth/issues/new?template=factual-error.yml).
- Provenance or citation concerns: Open a [Provenance Concern Report](https://github.com/innovatorved/system-design-in-depth/issues/new?template=provenance-concern.yml).
